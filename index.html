Chapter 012019

Where it started

Computer Engineering, University of Gondar · Hult Prize Campus Director

I studied Computer Engineering at the University of Gondar. As Hult Prize Campus Director, I led innovation and social-entrepreneurship programs for more than 1,000 students and mentored the teams that competed.

What I learnedTechnology matters most when it solves a real problem for real people.
Chapter 022024

Learning to protect

Talent Trainee, Information Network Security Administration (INSA)

I was selected as a National Digital Talent by INSA. I built security automation and audit tools in Go and Python, and set up monitoring that kept critical infrastructure healthy.

What I learnedBuild it secure from day one, and make it observable.
Chapter 032025

Founding Endebeto Tech

Founder · AI-enabled tourism platform

I founded Endebeto Tech to show the world Ethiopia the way travellers want to experience it. Our AI-enabled platform turns destinations, culture and local hosts into personalised, bookable journeys.
